DLavie Shader

A long-term Minecraft Bedrock shader/resource-pack project built around the official Vibrant Visuals and PBR pipeline.

The visual direction targets cinematic clarity, expressive skies, soft lighting, terrain-aware volumetric light shafts, and a vanilla-faithful PBR material feel while remaining designed for Bedrock mobile.

What makes it different

  • Vanilla-faithful materials. PBR enriches the original texture identity instead of replacing it.
  • Terrain-aware godrays. Sun shafts can form through leaves, windows, roofs, cave openings, and narrow terrain gaps.
  • Four real quality presets. Low, Medium, High, and Ultra use actual resource overrides rather than fake toggles.
  • Mobile first. Visual features are designed with sensible performance tiers and thermal limits in mind.

Install

  1. Download a trusted .mcpack build.
  2. Open it with Minecraft Bedrock.
  3. Activate DLavie Shader in Global Resources or the world resource packs.
  4. Enable Vibrant Visuals in Video settings.
  5. Choose Low, Medium, High, or Ultra from Pack Settings.
CURRENT

0.1.2 — Godrays Pass

  • Added vanilla-preserving volumetric fog generation and terrain-aware light shafts.
  • Added Low / Medium / High / Ultra volumetric profiles.
  • Reworked Mie scattering and glare into time-of-day keyframes.
  • Added Henyey-Greenstein forward scattering for sharper sunlight shafts.
  • Kept Nether and End behavior dimension-correct.

  • Fixed .mcpack import structure and root-manifest verification.
  • Kept stable manifest v2 subpack configuration.

Foundation

  • Initial Vibrant Visuals + PBR architecture.
  • Four quality presets, water, caustics, shadows, local lighting, and biome preservation.
  • Added validation, build tooling, architecture docs, and roadmap.
